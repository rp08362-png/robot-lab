ROBOT LAB V1.11 — IN-APP MANUAL READER

NEW
- Manuals are now readable inside ROBOT LAB, not just stored.
- PDF: full in-app page reader with previous/next and zoom.
- DOCX: rendered inside ROBOT LAB.
- ODT: text/document reader.
- RTF: text reader.
- TXT: text reader.
- Markdown: formatted reader.
- HTML: sanitized document reader.
- Android Back returns from a manual to the previous ROBOT LAB screen.
- Open Original remains available when useful.
- Legacy .DOC is stored but should be converted to DOCX/PDF for private in-app reading.
- Reader uses authenticated private file URLs and does not send manuals to external online document viewers.
